NPT Radio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Introduction

NPT Radio is an independent community-focused online radio project serving Neath, Port Talbot and surrounding communities. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how personal information is handled.

2. Information We Collect

You can browse our website without creating an account. Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs for website delivery and security.

If you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address and the contents of your message.

3. How Information Is Used

Where applicable, information is used to operate the website, respond to enquiries, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Processing will rely on an appropriate lawful basis under UK data protection law.

4. Browser Storage and Cookies

Our website uses browser local storage to save certain station settings and editor content on your device. You can clear this through your browser settings.

Hosting and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. Additional information and consent controls will be provided where legally required.

5. BBC News and External Links

Our website displays BBC Wales news headlines and links to external news, event and public-information websites. These third-party websites have their own privacy policies and may process information when you visit them.

6. Radio Streaming

NPT Radio is developing its internet radio service. When streaming becomes available, the streaming provider may process connection information to deliver audio. We will update this policy when the arrangements are known.

7. Sharing and Retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website or disclosed where required by law.

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose.

8. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where applicable.

You can also raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Children

NPT Radio is intended for a general community audience and does not knowingly operate a service designed to collect personal information directly from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website and services develop. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact NPT Radio using the contact details published on our website.
